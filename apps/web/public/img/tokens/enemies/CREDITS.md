# Default portrait artwork

Exactly two goblins and two humans are packaged with the app: Goblin Spearman, Goblin Guard, Human Swordsman and Human Mage.

All four were generated for this project using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then resized and framed for tokens. These are generated illustrations, not works attributed to a human artist. No Wesnoth or Lorestrome artwork is included.

## Prompt set

Shared direction: one square bust portrait; traditional pen and ink with sparse watercolor washes, economical contours, broad muted colors, limited shading, visible paper, subtle facial asymmetry. No text or border. Avoid glossy rendering, dense tiny detail and chibi styling.

- Spearman: lean green goblin, brown hood, spear, mossy ruins and blue forest with amber lantern.
- Guard: stocky olive goblin, battered iron helmet, burgundy padded armor, stone gate and purple hills with torchlight.
- Swordsman: weathered middle-aged human, dark graying hair, short beard, broken nose, plain cuirass and blue cloak; castle battlements and autumn hills.
- Mage: human woman with warm brown skin, curly dark hair, plum traveling robe; observatory arch, turquoise night sky and candlelight.

The PNG files in `source/` are the packaged source images; SVG files embed these images and add the circular token frame. The generated assets are distributed with the project; no third-party portrait license is required.
