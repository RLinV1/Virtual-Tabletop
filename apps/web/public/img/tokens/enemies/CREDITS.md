# Default portrait artwork

Exactly two goblins and two humans are packaged with the app.

## Humans

Human Traveler and Human Scout are by **Hyptosis**, from [200 Free Lorestrome Portraits](https://opengameart.org/content/200-free-lorestrome-portraits), published May 7, 2017 under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Source sheet: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/portraits_1.png
Selected 300 x 300 regions (x, y): Traveler (300, 4800); Scout (300, 0).
The original colored artwork and backgrounds are preserved; the app adds a circular crop and border.

## Goblins

Goblin Spearman and Goblin Guard were generated for this project using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then resized and framed for tokens. They are generated illustrations, not works attributed to a human artist. No Wesnoth artwork is included.

Prompt direction: one square goblin bust portrait; traditional pen and ink with sparse watercolor washes, economical contours, muted colors, limited shading, no text or border. Spearman: lean green goblin, brown hood, spear, mossy ruins and blue forest with amber lantern. Guard: stocky olive goblin, battered iron helmet, burgundy padded armor, stone gate and purple hills with torchlight. Avoid glossy rendering, dense tiny detail and chibi styling.

The PNG files in `source/` are the packaged source images; SVG files embed these images and add the circular token frame.
