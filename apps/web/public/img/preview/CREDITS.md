# Animation preview artwork

The animation editor's preview stage and the library's animation thumbnails use three pictures:
`arena.webp` (the courtyard map), `attacker.webp` and `target.webp` (the two stand-in portraits).

All three were generated for this project with ChatGPT's built-in image generation, then cropped
for round tokens and resized. These are generated illustrations, not works attributed to a human
artist. No other project artwork was reused for them.

## Prompt set

Shared direction: "The Proving Ground", a ruined dueling courtyard at night. Moody painterly
dark-fantasy concept art, cool moonlight from the top-left, dark and desaturated so bright spell
effects stand out. No text, frame or watermark.

- Arena: top-down orthographic battle map, open slate floor with a faint carved rune circle in the
  centre, ruins and two ember braziers only around the edges, no grid.
- Attacker: bust portrait of a spellblade in a teal cloak with a sword, arrows and a glowing blue
  rune, facing right.
- Target: bust portrait of a basalt training golem bound in iron, ember-red visor and cracks,
  facing left.
